# insult - "In this world, every insult comes true, until you say sorry."
I saved my sister's life by calling her an airhead. Literally.
You see, in this world, every insult comes true. Poof! And it stays that way until you say sorry. So everybody is super polite. Teachers never call anyone lazy, or the kid would melt into the chair. Football coaches never yell, "You're slow!" Because the whole team would turn into turtles. And if you call someone a pig at school, you'd better have a muddy field nearby.
I haven't insulted anyone since I was five. That year, I called my grandpa an old fossil, and he spent a week in the museum next to a T-Rex. He liked it there. Too much.
Kids at school know I'll never fight back. "Nerd!" Poof! Glasses. "Shorty!" Poof! Two inches gone. "Loser!" Poof! A big L on my forehead. I said nothing. Every day.
But one night, our house caught fire. My big sister was trapped on the roof. The firefighters' ladder was too short. The flames were climbing. She screamed, "Do something!"
Then I noticed something. Insults don't just hurt you. They change you. And maybe, they could save her.
I took a deep breath and yelled the meanest thing I'd ever said. "You're such a chicken!" Poof! She turned into a chicken. She flapped. Flap, flap, flap! But chickens can't really fly. She was falling. "You're an airhead!" Poomp! Her head blew up like a giant balloon. She floated in the air like a parade float. But the wind started pulling her right back into the smoke. "You're as dumb as a rock!" Bonk! She turned to stone and dropped straight down onto the firefighters' trampoline. Boing! She was safe. A rock, a chicken, and a balloon. But safe.
Then came the hardest part. "I'm sorry you're a chicken." "I'm sorry you're an airhead." "And I'm really sorry you're a rock." Poof! Poof! Poof! My sister was back. She hugged me so hard, my glasses fell off. Then she smiled and said, "Thanks, nerd." Poof! New glasses.
