# dad: every dad can freeze time while telling a boring story
My dad stopped a bank robbery by talking about his garage for 11 minutes.
You see, in this world, every dad can freeze time,
but only while he's telling a boring story.
The moment the story gets interesting, click! Time starts again.
So every dad has trained for years.
They tell stories about parking spots, about lawn fertilizer.
About the time a different road was closed.
Dad meetings are the quietest places on earth because the whole world is frozen solid.
And my dad, he's the best in the country.
Boring champion three years in a row.
His record is four hours about a screwdriver.
Everyone in my family hates it.
Mom's eyes glaze over.
My sister once fell asleep standing up.
And me? I rolled my eyes so hard, I got a headache.
Dad, please stop.
Well kiddo, it all started in 1994 when I bought a garage door.
Frozen.
Birds in the air.
Mom's coffee hanging in the air.
Dad just kept going.
But one Saturday, we went to the bank.
Suddenly, three masked robbers burst in.
Everybody on the ground!
Guns up, bags open, everyone screamed.
My little sister was standing right in front of them, holding a balloon.
One robber pointed at her.
You! Come here!
I looked at dad.
He was shaking.
And I whispered, Dad, tell the garage story.
He took a deep breath.
Well, it all started in 1994.
FOOM!
Silence!
The robbers froze mid-yell.
The balloon froze mid-float.
Dad kept talking.
And the door was beige.
Not cream, not white.
Beige.
I ran, grabbed my sister, and pulled her away.
Mom tied the robbers up with the bank's velvet ropes.
A security guard stuffed their pockets with candy, just for fun.
But dad's voice was getting hoarse.
And then, the remote needed batteries.
He yawned.
He was running out of boring.
Then I noticed something.
If I made the story more interesting, time would start again.
But if I made it more boring...
Dad, I said.
Tell me about the batteries.
The exact brand, the expiration date, what they cost.
His eyes lit up.
He had never been asked before.
Well, kiddo.
Time stayed frozen.
Eleven minutes.
Then the police arrived and dad finally stopped.
Click!
The robbers woke up in handcuffs, completely confused.
That night, dad sat on my bed.
Thanks for asking about the batteries.
I smiled.
Tell me more.
Well, it all started in 1994.
And this time, I didn't roll my eyes.
