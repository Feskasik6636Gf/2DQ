# story - "In this world, every time you sneeze, you teleport to whatever you were thinking about."
Every sneeze teleports you. Last week I sneezed once, and I ended up in the middle of a frozen forest, holding a cat. Let me explain.
In this world, every time you sneeze, you teleport to whatever you were thinking about. Whatever you're holding comes with you. So pepper is sold only with a prescription. In spring, airports are empty, because why fly when you can just sniff a flower? Bless you means goodbye.
My grandma once sneezed while holding her couch. She landed in Paris, still sitting on it. My dad sneezed thinking about a sandwich. He woke up inside a sandwich shop in Ohio, wearing pajamas. And at my uncle's wedding, the groom sneezed during the vows while thinking about his ex. There was no wedding.
I'm Sam. I have the worst allergies in town. Cats, dust, flowers, everything. I've taken pills every morning since I was four. The year I sneezed thinking about a giraffe and woke up in a zoo in Kenya. I haven't sneezed since.
Then one night, my little sister Mia chased a stray cat into the woods. A snowstorm hit. The search teams couldn't find her. Nobody could. But I didn't need to know where she was. I just needed to think about her and sneeze.
My pills were still working. My nose was dead. So I broke into grandma's secret pepper drawer, opened the whole grinder, and breathed it in like a vacuum cleaner. My eyes watered. My brain screamed. I thought about Mia's face as hard as I could. Achoo!
Snow. Darkness. And right in front of me, Mia, shivering, hugging that stray cat. She smiled. I knew you'd come. But I had no pepper left. No way home. Then I looked at the cat. I'm allergic to cats. I grabbed Mia, grabbed the cat, and pushed my face right into its fur. I thought about our kitchen. Achoo!
We landed right on the kitchen table, in front of Mom. Mom cried and hugged us so hard I could barely breathe. We kept the cat. Now I sneeze forty times a day. Yesterday, I thought about pizza. I'm writing this from Italy.
