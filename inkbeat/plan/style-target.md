## Look (from 24 sampled frames)
- Characters: plain white/cream ROUND heads, big expressive faces (brows, sweat drops, moustaches), thin stick
  limbs, cloth bodies in the faction colour (red vs teal), crowns/helmets, thin dark ink outline.
- Two factions colour-coded everywhere (red / teal): flags, cloaks, shields, ground markings, soldier blocks.
- Sets are layered and DENSE: foreground props (anvil, barrels, shield, helmets with flowers), mid-ground
  characters, far castles + mountains in haze. Painted gradients on stone, wood, grass; strong cast shadows.
- Heroes fill 35-45% of the frame height, low lens, foreground object in a corner (soft).
- Overhead / top-down shots of armies and terrain as an establishing beat; day-night time-lapse for "time passes".
- Painted counter on a wooden board in the world ("YEARS AT WAR 099 -> 100"), not a floating card.
- Captions: white, 1 word at a time, centred ~70% height, heavy dark stroke.
- Light: saturated blue sky with big soft cumulus, warm key, readable cool shadows.
