In 1859, America and Britain almost went to war over a pig.
It happened on a tiny island both countries claimed.
An American farmer woke up and found a huge black pig eating his potatoes. He shot it.
The pig belonged to a British company, and they demanded his arrest.
He refused.
Soon, hundreds of American soldiers landed on the island.
Britain answered with five warships and over two thousand men.
Cannons stared at each other across a field of potatoes.
But both commanders got the same order: do not fire first.
So nobody did.
They camped there for twelve years, even sharing parties and holidays.
In 1872, a German emperor settled it, and the island became American.
The only casualty of the Pig War?
One pig.
